# Coordination of Benefits: Using Two Health Plans for Physiotherapy

If you are covered by your own health plan and also by a spouse's plan, you can usually claim from both. This is called coordination of benefits. The plans do not pay twice; the second plan considers what the first one left unpaid.

The steps below follow Manitoba Blue Cross's published guidance. Other insurers may word their rules differently, so confirm with yours.

## Which plan pays first

**For your own visits:** submit the claim to your own plan first. When it sends back a statement showing what it paid, send that statement and the original claim to your spouse's plan for the unpaid balance.

**For your spouse's visits:** the order is reversed. Their own plan goes first, then yours.

**For children covered by both parents:** the plan of the parent whose birthday falls earlier in the calendar year goes first. This is based on the month and day, not on who is older. If both birthdays are the same, Manitoba Blue Cross says to go in alphabetical order of first name.

**A health spending account goes last.** Manitoba Blue Cross states that a health spending account must be the last payer, after all other plans have been billed.

Blended families and other arrangements have their own rules. Manitoba Blue Cross refers members to the Canadian Life and Health Insurance Association for those.

## How this works with direct billing

When a clinic direct bills, it sends the claim to your first plan for you. Tell reception before your visit if you have a second plan, and bring the details for both. Ask whether the clinic can submit to the second plan or whether you send that claim yourself with the first plan's statement.

Direct billing does not guarantee payment. Each plan decides what it covers.

## What to check in each plan

Your plan booklet or your insurer's member site will show:

- **The annual maximum** for physiotherapy and how much remains.
- **The per-visit limit**, as a percentage or a fixed amount.
- **The benefit year.** Each plan sets its own start and end dates.
- **Whether a doctor's referral is required** before the plan reimburses physiotherapy. You can book physiotherapy in Manitoba without one, but some plans ask for it.

Your physiotherapist recommends treatment based on your assessment, not on what your plan covers. Knowing your coverage simply tells you what you may need to pay yourself.
