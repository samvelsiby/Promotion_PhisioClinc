# Pro Motion brand refresh

## Direction

Source of truth: `public/navbar/Navbar.svg` uses red **#EC1C24** and grey **#58595B**. The old muted #B53632 was not the logo red. Updated shared tokens use logo red, white #FFFFFF, graphite #25262A, neutral grey #F6F7F8. #C8101E is a darker accessible red for small links and hover states, not a substitute logo colour. White text on the vivid red uses large bold labels.

Navigation has a graphite theme and is a single 80px row (72px on phones), with readable service links, a clear active state, telephone access and one booking action. The existing Juvonno destination is preserved. The hero is entirely white with no split-colour treatment. Interior sections use white and neutral grey rather than cream or pink. Navigation and booking bands use graphite; brand red is reserved for actions and small accents. No copied layouts or imported Mobbin assets.

Staff images are bounded to 230px wide in the team directory, 290px on desktop profiles, and 150px alongside credentials on the homepage. The main desktop hero photograph is capped at 460px high rather than 590–650px minimum height.

## Mobbin references inspected

- [Superpower navigation](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/570d1933-5cc4-430a-9d4a-8cf67b35403f): compact horizontal navigation and a visually distinct primary action. Adopt the hierarchy, not the dark floating pill or full-screen image.
- [Maxima Therapy](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/fc167e3e-e2f2-4340-bfcf-52cc593edfbb): saturated brand fields with white content surfaces can feel energetic. Its multi-colour illustrations and playful wordmark do not fit this clinic.
- [Hims corporate header](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/9ade11e2-61a0-4e63-a2c8-a093c9bd3ac0): white navigation is legible against colour. The screenshot contains little body content, so it is only a colour/header reference.
- [The Performance Lab team](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/c74d5ee3-7a45-4f29-b4c5-096f1d13ee9e): restrained headshot size with clinician credentials and consultation access given clear space.
- [Headspace clinical leadership](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b7535e50-c916-4fd4-b12b-1c11b73b63ce): small portraits support the names and qualifications rather than dominating the section.

Other retrieved Hims and Superpower hero examples were inspected but their immersive lifestyle imagery was unsuitable for the requested smaller photography. One Analogue Agency result was blank and supplied no useful design evidence.
