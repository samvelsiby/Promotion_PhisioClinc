# Pro-Motion Google Business Profile review — 28 September 2026

## What the two Google tools measure

Google Search Console reports how the **website** appears in Google Search: queries, pages, impressions, clicks, indexing, and related diagnostics. Google Business Profile (GBP) controls the clinic’s **Search and Maps listing**: name, category, address, hours, services, booking link, photos, and other listing information. GBP Performance reports profile views and interactions such as call-button clicks, direction requests and website-link clicks. Search Console clicks should not be treated as all GBP calls or direction requests; compare the two reports separately.

## Proposed edits after physiotherapist approval

1. Set exact business name to **Pro-Motion Physiotherapy**.
2. Remove all GBP service areas because the clinic does not visit patients at their locations. Keep the clinic address displayed.
3. Add the clinic’s existing Facebook page as a social profile. Check that Google Maps' directions route to the clinic's street entrance; the website's updated link uses the owner-supplied full address and tells visitors to look for Unit 4 and the front parking area.
4. Review the GBP services list against the services actually offered on the website and by registered clinicians. Candidate labels for physiotherapist review: **physiotherapy assessment**, **back and neck pain physiotherapy**, **sports injury rehabilitation**, **post-surgical rehabilitation**, **motor vehicle injury physiotherapy**, and **workplace injury physiotherapy**. Add only services the clinic actually offers; do not use condition names as unsupported practice-specialty claims. Google supports a dedicated services editor, separate from business categories.
5. Check holiday hours before the next holiday and use authentic clinic/exterior/team photos only with appropriate consent. Do not create or request patient testimonials.

The current description already explains the clinic, location, services, and booking clearly. There is no evidence that rewriting it for more keywords would help. Google says local results depend mainly on relevance, distance, and popularity; profile completeness and accuracy matter, but no ranking position is guaranteed.

The [College of Physiotherapists of Manitoba Advertising and Marketing Practice Direction](https://manitobaphysio.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Advertising-and-Marketing.pdf) says a physiotherapist is accountable for information on clinic websites and other online marketing platforms and must review and approve third-party marketing. The owner confirmed review and approval of the two neck and wrist posts, and they were published. This confirmation did not specifically cover the proposed GBP edits; keep those pending review.
