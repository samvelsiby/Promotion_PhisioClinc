# Ad approval request for the physiotherapist

The College of Physiotherapists of Manitoba holds the physiotherapist accountable for all clinic advertising and requires them to review and approve marketing prepared by a third party. This is the email to send to Ben or Lijo. Their reply is the record: save it, and send a new one whenever the wording changes.

Copy everything below the line into an email.

---

**Subject:** Please review and approve: Google ads for Pro-Motion Physiotherapy

Hi [Ben / Lijo],

Before any Google ad runs, the College requires a physiotherapist at the clinic to review and approve the wording. Everything we plan to use is listed below. Please read it and reply with one of:

- "I have reviewed the keywords, ad text and pages listed and I approve them", or
- the lines you want changed or removed.

Please check each line is true for the clinic today. In particular: the hours, direct billing, MPI and WCB, parking, and "no referral needed".

**1. Where and when the ads show**

- Google Search only. No banner ads, no ads that follow people around, no patient lists shared with Google.
- People within about 6 km of 567 St. Anne's Rd, during clinic hours.

**2. Searches the ads appear for**

- Clinic name: pro-motion physiotherapy, promotion physio winnipeg, pro motion physio st annes
- Local: physiotherapy st vital, st vital physiotherapy, physio near me, physiotherapist near me, physiotherapy winnipeg, physio winnipeg
- Hours: physio open sunday, physio open saturday, evening physiotherapy, same day physiotherapy, walk-in physio clinic
- Claims and billing: car accident physiotherapy, mpi physiotherapy, whiplash treatment, wcb physiotherapy, work injury physio, physiotherapy direct billing
- Conditions (added later): back pain, sciatica, neck pain, shoulder, knee pain and TMJ physiotherapy

We will not advertise on other clinics' names, or for dry needling, acupuncture, cupping, orthotics, pelvic floor, massage or occupational therapy.

**3. Headlines**

1. Physiotherapy in St. Vital
2. Pro-Motion Physiotherapy
3. Open 7 Days a Week
4. Evening Appointments to 8 PM
5. Saturday & Sunday Hours
6. Book Your Assessment Online
7. Direct Billing Available
8. MPI & WCB Claim Support

10. Registered Physiotherapists
11. 567 St. Anne's Rd, Winnipeg

13. Physio After a Car Accident
14. Parking at the Clinic Door

**4. Descriptions**

1. Assessment and treatment by registered physiotherapists on St. Anne's Road. Book online.
2. Open weekdays to 8 PM and on weekends. Call the clinic or book your assessment online.
3. Direct billing to participating insurers. Coverage depends on your plan. MPI & WCB claims.
4. Your first visit includes an assessment, a treatment plan and a home exercise program.

**5. Extras shown with the ad**

- The clinic's address and phone number
- Links to: First Visit, Insurance and Direct Billing, MPI Claims, Meet the Team

**6. Pages the ads lead to**

- Home page
- First Visit
- Insurance and Direct Billing
- Motor vehicle accident (MPI) page
- Work injury (WCB) page
- Later: the back pain, neck, shoulder, knee and TMJ pages

Please look at these pages on the website as well, since they count as advertising too.

**7. What the ads will never say**

No "best", "leading" or "#1". No "specialist", "expert" or "advanced". No promises of results. No free assessments, discounts or packages. No requests for reviews.
