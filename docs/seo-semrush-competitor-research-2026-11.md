# Semrush competitor and keyword research

Written 21 November 2026. Data is from Semrush's Canadian database, pulled the same day. Semrush estimates are national and undercount small local sites, so treat volumes as relative size, not exact counts. All recommendations still have to pass the College rules in `docs/treatment-pages-compliance-2026-11.md`.

## Summary

1. Pro-Motion's link profile is close to the main St. Vital competitor and far behind the three city-wide ones. The local race is winnable; the city-wide one is not yet.
2. Treatment pages are the largest gap. The four drafts already built cover about 2,200 searches a month, and every page-one result for "dry needling winnipeg" is a dedicated treatment page.
3. The clinic is open seven days and until 8 PM on weekdays, but the site does not rank for the "open Sunday", "open Saturday" or "open today" searches that the St. Vital competitor wins from its homepage.
4. The Manitoba Physiotherapy Association directory links to all four competitors and not to Pro-Motion. It also ranks on page one itself for the main local searches.

## Backlinks

Of the 30 sites that link to all four competitors and not to Pro-Motion, 27 are link spam (junk `.top`, `.sbs`, `.monster` domains) and should be ignored. Three are real:

| Site | Authority score | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| mbphysio.org | 26 | Manitoba Physiotherapy Association clinic directory. I loaded its Find A Physio page: 137 clinics listed, including both St. Vital competitors, and no Pro-Motion entry. |
| clinicdirectory.ca | 8 | Clinic directory |
| physiotherapynear.ca | 6 | Clinic directory |

## What to do, in order

1. **Publish the treatment pages** once the clinic signs off the checklist in the compliance doc. Largest measured gap.
2. **Get listed on mbphysio.org.** This is a clinic task: it likely needs association membership. One listing gives a relevant link and a place on a page that already ranks.
3. **Put opening hours where Google and patients see them.** The clinic is open Monday to Friday 9 AM to 8 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 4 PM and Sunday 10 AM to 4 PM. State "open 7 days, evenings and weekends" in the homepage title, description and first screen. It is a verifiable fact, so it is allowed.
4. **Fix the back pain mix-up.** Google is ranking the neck pain page for "back pain winnipeg". Tighten titles, headings and internal links so the back pain page owns back pain terms.
5. **Rework the TMJ, motor vehicle and elbow pages** against the pages that outrank them. All three terms are low difficulty.
6. **Continue the community-centre hub.** Prairie Trail's walking-trails post shows a local-interest page can rank for a 2,400-a-month term.

## What not to do

- Do not target "best physiotherapist winnipeg" or similar. Prairie Trail ranks first for it, but the College bars superlatives.
- Do not buy or chase the spam links competitors have.
- Do not build pages for services the clinic does not provide.

## Limits of this research

- Semrush showed Pro-Motion outside the top 10 for "dry needling winnipeg" while Search Console showed an average position of 4. The two measure differently: Search Console averages real impressions, often from nearby searchers; Semrush samples one national result page.
- I did not pull full keyword lists for physiowinnipeg.com or winnipegphysio.com, only their top 30 page-one keywords.
- I did not run Semrush's keyword gap report. The competitor keyword lists above did the same job at lower cost.
- The mbphysio.org check covered the clinics on one page load. It may not be the full directory.

## API units used
