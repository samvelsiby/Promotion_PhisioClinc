# Landing page direction

The supplied references inform the design rather than supplying clinic claims or copy.

- Reference 1: large photographic hero, confident colour fields, clear mobile hierarchy.
- Reference 2: editorial serif typography and a gentle green treatment section.
- Reference 3: prominent booking and phone access, readable service navigation.

Palette: cobalt #245AE8, ink #142E46, ice #EDF3FF, mint #E5F2EB, peach #FFF0E7, existing logo red #EC1C24.
Typography: Source Sans 3 for navigation, body, and the opening statement; Cormorant Garamond for editorial headings and the second hero sentence.

Layout: left-aligned copy and treatment photography side by side on desktop; copy, booking, photograph, and trust information stacked on phones. Service entries combine small photographs with plain-language condition names. Treatment, reviews, and practical information use different colour fields to avoid a repetitive monochrome page.

Research: https://www.kidd.co/work/your-physio/ — blue/red physiotherapy identity and strong typography; https://bragdeal.com/project/physio2u/ — mobile-oriented presentation and varied colour palette. These are design references, not performance evidence.

Assets: existing local treatment photography and staff portraits retained. Original lightweight vector movement-lines.svg created for the closing section; no new synthetic people or stock downloads required.

Validation: TypeScript no-emit passes. Browser verified menu open/close and FAQ expansion. No horizontal overflow at 320, 390, and 1440 pixels. Desktop and 390-pixel hero visually inspected. Preview runs locally; not published.
