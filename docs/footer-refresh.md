# Footer refresh

Only the shared footer is changed. The current hero, its founding-physiotherapist description, existing booking system, and all page bodies are preserved.

## Mobbin research

Reviewed the actual footer images returned by Mobbin:

- [Superpower Health Gap](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/29f16f47-5d3f-43b7-a885-bee6eb9b2ead): bright background and clearly grouped navigation.
- [Function](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/86b57046-171c-45b5-80e5-61e86bc6747c): separate navigation, social, and legal/credit areas. Its cream palette and newsletter treatment were not used.
- [YLLW](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/71ec7c29-2e27-4702-ac7b-b26ccea5d8a0): a distinct appointment prompt above the footer navigation.

The resulting layout is an original synthesis for a local clinic, with no copied assets or individual design.

## Direction

Use the existing Outfit typeface, white #ffffff, subtle gray #f9fafb, dark heading gray #111827, body gray #4b5563, borders #e5e7eb, and accessible brand-family red #c8101e for booking. Separate a concise booking row from four desktop columns: brand/contact, treatments, patient information, and clinic location. On phones, contact and location span the width, with two compact navigation columns underneath. Keep a small lazy-loaded map rather than the former full-width 400px embed.

## Local search and usability

- Reuse COMPANY_CONTACT and NAP_LINE for address, phone, email, social accounts, booking destination, and opening hours.
- Sunday now displays the shared-data opening time of 10 AM, matching the existing structured data instead of the former footer's 9 AM.
- Retain all nine treatment destinations with readable descriptive labels.
- Consolidate duplicate team/about/services links and remove the previously broken FAQ fragment from the footer.
- Include phone, email, directions, Google reviews, and a clearly labeled booking action.
- Preserve the RTSTICS credit, visible focus styles, named navigation landmarks, and a titled lazy-loaded map. Footer logo is no longer priority-loaded.
- Keep the existing global clinic schema instead of adding duplicate structured data.

These improvements support navigation and consistent local information; no ranking or conversion increase is claimed without measurement.
