- Candidate searches: TMJ physiotherapy Winnipeg, jaw pain physiotherapy.
- Reader need: understand the assessment process and when to consult another professional.
- Outline: describe symptoms and everyday limitations; relevant dental/medical history; questions about assessment and referral; what an individualized plan involves; booking in St. Vital.

- Candidate searches: shoulder pain physiotherapy Winnipeg, shoulder pain lifting arm.

## 3. Heel pain on your first steps: questions to ask your physiotherapist
- Candidate searches: heel pain Winnipeg, plantar fasciitis physiotherapy Winnipeg.
- Reader need: understand what information helps assess heel pain, without assuming all heel pain is plantar fasciitis.
- Outline: symptom location and timing; recent activity and footwear changes; questions about possible causes and care options; individualized activity guidance; follow-up expectations.
- Link to an existing relevant foot/ankle service only if the clinic confirms that service.

## 4. Knee pain on stairs: what to bring up at your first appointment
- Candidate searches: knee pain physiotherapy Winnipeg, knee pain stairs.
- Reader need: prepare for assessment of everyday knee limitations.
- Outline: stairs and other difficult tasks; injury history; changes in swelling or movement to report; assessment questions; goals and progress review.
- Distinguish from the existing ACL article: this is about undiagnosed everyday symptoms, not ligament reconstruction or return-to-play timelines.

## 5. After an ankle sprain: questions about returning to walking, work and sport
- Candidate searches: ankle sprain physiotherapy Winnipeg, ankle sprain return to sport.
- Reader need: understand which questions to ask about safe return to activity.
- Outline: prior medical assessment and injury history; walking and work demands; how readiness is assessed; personalized rehabilitation questions; when to seek reassessment.
- Link to the existing sports-injury service after verifying the route. Do not give a universal recovery timeline or home exercise prescription.
