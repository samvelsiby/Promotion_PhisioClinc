# Community-centre SEO strategy and ranking review: November 2026

Data: Google Search Console, `sc-domain:promotionphysiotherapy.ca`, 22 August to 19 November 2026 (90 days). Keyword ideas are Google autocomplete suggestions for Canada, which show what people type but not volumes. The community centre list comes from the General Council of Winnipeg Community Centres (GCWCC) directory.

## The short version

1. **The community-centre angle is already the best thing on the blog.** The eight local posts published on 28 October earned 15 clicks and about 1,400 impressions in their first 23 days. The Dakota Community Centre post alone has 283 impressions, 6 clicks and an average position of 8.6.
2. **The real prize is "physiotherapy St. Vital".** Three variants of that search add up to about 220 impressions at positions 11 to 15, with zero clicks. They are one push away from page 1. Community-centre content is the most natural way to build the local relevance that moves them.
3. **81% of the clicks Google attributes to a named search are brand searches.** Only 42 non-brand clicks in 90 days can be tied to a query. Growth has to come from non-brand local searches.
4. **Service pages are the weakest part of the site.** Every one sits between position 33 and 64.

> **Correction, 21 November 2026:** point 1 overstated the blog. The blog figures in this doc count every country. Split by country, blog posts earned 54 clicks worldwide and 1 click in Canada over the 90 days. The Dakota post has 97 impressions, 1 click and position 9.3 in Canada; the hockey groin post has 4 Canadian impressions. The community-centre direction still holds, on Semrush volumes rather than on these clicks. See docs/blog-plan-2026-11.md.

> Compliance note, added 20 November: all marketing for the clinic falls under the College of Physiotherapists of Manitoba advertising rules. See docs/treatment-pages-compliance-2026-11.md before acting on any item here.

### What it will and will not do

Someone searching "dakota community centre schedule" wants the schedule, not a physiotherapist. Few of those visitors will book on that visit. The value is in three other places:

- **Local relevance.** Pages about real St. Vital places tell Google the clinic belongs to St. Vital. That supports the "physiotherapy st vital" searches, which are the ones that bring patients.
- **The right audience.** People who use these centres are active, live within a few kilometres and get the injuries the clinic treats.
- **Local links and mentions.** Community centres, leagues and clubs have sponsor pages, newsletters and event listings. These are the most relevant local links the clinic can get.

One caution: the "dakota community centre" search shows position 1 in the data, but on a single impression. That is not a stable ranking yet.

## Part 3: The strategy

### 1. One hub page

Create `/community` as a proper site page (not a blog post), linked from the footer and from a short "In the community" section on the homepage.

Working title: **Staying Active in St. Vital: Community Centres, Rinks and Courts Near St. Anne's Road**

Contents: a short introduction, one card per centre linking to its guide, one card per activity linking to its injury guide, a seasonal "what's on now" block, and a booking call to action. Target searches: "st vital community centre", "community centre winnipeg south", "st vital recreation centre".

### 2. Centre guides (spokes)

Publish in this order. Confirm each centre's catchment and facilities on its own website before writing.

| Priority | Centre | Why | Angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dakota Community Centre (exists) | Already ranking | Expand: fieldhouse hours and open gym, 55+ programs, stick and puck, directions to the clinic from 1188 Dakota Street |
| 2 | Southdale Community Centre | "physiotherapy sage creek" has 48 impressions at position 60; Southdale is the closest centre to that area | Seniors programs, rinks, getting to the clinic from Southdale, Island Lakes and Sage Creek |
| 3 | St. Vital Centennial Arena | "public skating" and "schedule" are searched | Public skating, falls on ice, wrist and ankle injuries |
| 4 | Glenwood, Norberry-Glenlee and Greendell Park (one combined guide) | Smaller centres in older St. Vital | Outdoor rinks, youth sport, neighbourhood directions |
| 5 | Windsor and Winakwa (one combined guide) | Windsor Park is the next neighbourhood east | Gym, soccer, hockey |

Each guide needs its own content, at least 600 words, with no paragraphs reused between guides:

- What is at the centre, linked to the centre's own pages
- What is running this season
- The injuries that go with those activities, linked to the matching condition post
- How to get from the centre to the clinic, with a drive time checked on Google Maps
- Three or four FAQs taken from the autocomplete suggestions
- Booking call to action

Stop at five guides. A long run of near-identical "physio near X" pages is what Google's spam policy calls doorway pages, and it would put the whole site at risk.

### 4. Internal linking rules

- Every guide links to the hub, to one service page and to the homepage.
- Vary the homepage link text: "physiotherapy in St. Vital", "our St. Vital physiotherapy clinic", "physiotherapist on St. Anne's Road".
- The two existing St. Vital blog posts (`choosing-physiotherapy-st-vital` at position 10.8 and `physiotherapy-st-annes-road-st-vital-neighbourhoods` at 9.9) compete with the homepage for the same searches. Add a clear link from each to the homepage near the top, so the homepage is the one page Google ranks for "physiotherapy st vital".
- Add a short "Local to St. Vital" paragraph on each service page linking to the hub.

### 5. Off-site: turn the content into real relationships

| Action | What it gives | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Dakota website and newsletter placement. The September outreach pack has the email drafted and the tracker still says "not contacted". Published rates were $320 for a three-month website placement and $160 for the newsletter; reconfirm. | Referral visits from the exact audience, a local mention | About $480 |
| Give a 20-minute educational talk at a centre: pickleball warm-up, staying steady on your feet, groin strain prevention for hockey. General education only. Do not advertise free screenings, assessments or balance checks, which the College's advertising rules prohibit. | Event listing on the centre's site and social, people met in person | Staff time |
| Sponsor one team or league (minor hockey, Bonivital Soccer Club, St. Vital Curling Club next season) | Listing on a sponsor page | Varies |
| QR card on centre notice boards pointing to `/community?utm_source=dakota&utm_medium=print` | Measurable offline traffic | Printing |
| Weekly Google Business Profile post tied to the centre calendar (registration, tournaments, first skate) | Fresh local signals on the profile | Staff time |

Paid placements should carry a sponsored link attribute. Their value is the referral traffic and the local association, not link equity.

### 6. Measurement

- Search Console: track a query filter for `dakota|community|pickleball|arena|skating|soccer|curling|seniors` and a second one for `st vital|st annes|meadowood|sage creek|southdale`.
- GA4: `booking_click` and `phone_click` need to be marked as key events if that has not been done since the October review. Without this there is no way to see which guide produces bookings.
- Reception: add "community centre" to the "how did you hear about us" options.
