## Campaign URL conventions

Example for a paid newsletter placement:

https://www.promotionphysiotherapy.ca/first-visit?utm_source=dakota&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=local_patient_information

Use a neutral campaign label. Never include a patient's identity, health information or insurance number in URLs. Canonical URLs should remain clean. UTM parameters only label traffic; they do not establish completed-booking attribution. Confirm what the actual analytics/booking setup can measure.
