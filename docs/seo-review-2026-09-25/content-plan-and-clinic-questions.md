## Writing and images

Open with the reader’s actual question. Use concrete examples: stairs, a work shift, lifting groceries, driving. Avoid “unlock”, “journey”, “game-changing”, dramatic injury stories and repeated city keywords. Cite clinical sources near the claims. Do not invent patient cases, quotations, staff authorship or review dates.

Prefer a real entrance/parking photograph for the local guide and a clinician-approved demonstration for an exercise article. Generated images are optional illustrations only; never present generated people as actual patients or clinicians. No image generation was needed for this audit.
