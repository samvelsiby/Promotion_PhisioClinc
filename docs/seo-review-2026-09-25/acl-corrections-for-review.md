## Replacement opening and core copy

# ACL injury recovery: what to discuss with your care team

After a knee injury, the practical questions can come quickly. Can you manage the stairs at home? What should you tell your employer? How will you know when you are ready to return to sport?

Start with your own assessment and care plan. A professional athlete’s recovery story cannot tell you what your knee needs.

## Does an ACL tear always mean surgery?

An ACL tear can be partial or complete. Treatment depends on how stable your knee is, any other injuries, and what you want to return to. Surgery may be recommended, particularly when instability affects demanding activities. Some people can be managed without surgery. Discuss the options, risks and likely demands of rehabilitation with your treating clinician and surgeon.

Source: [AAOS: ACL injury treatment options](https://www.orthoinfo.org/treatment/acl-injury-does-it-require-surgery).

## What is rehabilitation working towards?

Rehabilitation can address movement, swelling, strength and the tasks you need to do. Progression depends on your injury and, if you have had surgery, the procedure and instructions from your surgical team. Ask what you should practise now and what needs to improve before the next stage.

Returning to sport involves more than reaching a date on the calendar. Your care team should assess how your knee is functioning and explain what further preparation is needed.

Source: [AAOS: ACL rehabilitation](https://www.orthoinfo.org/treatment/acl-injury-does-it-require-surgery).

## Make your appointment useful

Bring any reports, surgical instructions or exercise plan you have already received. Make a short note of the tasks you find difficult and the activities you hope to return to. You do not need technical language.

Useful questions include:

- What are we working on first, and why?
- What should I do if the current plan makes symptoms worse?
- Which work or sport activities need to change for now?
- How will you check whether I am ready to progress?
- Who should I contact if I am unsure about an instruction?
